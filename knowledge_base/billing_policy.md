# The AI Demo Store – Billing and Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
The AI Demo Store accepts:
- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- UPI
- Net banking
- Supported digital wallets

## Payment Failures
- If payment fails, customers should check whether money was deducted.
- Customers should not repeatedly retry payment without checking the transaction status.
- Failed transactions with deducted amounts require payment verification.

## Duplicate Charges
- Customers reporting duplicate charges should provide their Order ID and transaction reference.
- Duplicate payment complaints must be reviewed by the billing team.
- AI support agents cannot independently issue refunds for duplicate charges.

## Billing Invoices
- Customers can download invoices from My Account > My Orders.
- Invoice correction requests must be reviewed by the billing team.
- Tax-related invoice disputes require human review.

## Payment Disputes
- Unauthorized transactions must be escalated immediately.
- Suspected payment fraud must be escalated to human support.
- Chargeback requests and disputed payments require billing-team investigation.

## Billing Security
- Customers must never share card PINs, CVVs, passwords, or OTPs.
- Support agents must not request sensitive payment credentials.
- AI support agents cannot perform financial transactions.
