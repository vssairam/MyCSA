# The AI Demo Store – Product FAQ and Replacement Policy

## Damaged Products

* Customers should report products damaged during delivery within 48 hours of receiving them.
* Customers should provide their Order ID and photographs of the damage.
* Damaged-product claims are reviewed by the returns team.
* Replacement or refund eligibility is determined after review.

## Wrong Product Delivered

* Customers should report incorrect products within 7 calendar days of delivery.
* Customers should provide their Order ID and photographs of the received product.
* The returns team will verify the issue before approving a replacement.

## Defective Products

* Products that stop working within the return window may qualify for replacement.
* Products outside the return window may be covered by the manufacturer's warranty.
* Warranty eligibility depends on the product and manufacturer.

## Replacement Process

1. Customer submits a replacement request.
2. Customer provides the Order ID and issue details.
3. The returns team reviews the request.
4. If approved, pickup or return instructions are provided.
5. Replacement dispatch begins after the return is verified.

## Replacement Timeline

* Approved replacements are normally dispatched within 3 business days after verification.
* Delivery follows the standard shipping timeline.
* Replacement availability depends on stock.

## Product Support

* Customers can contact support for product specifications and warranty information.
* AI support agents cannot guarantee replacement approval or stock availability.
