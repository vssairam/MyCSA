# The AI Demo Store – Account and Login Support

## Password Reset

Customers who forget their password should:

1. Open The AI Demo Store login page.
2. Click Forgot Password.
3. Enter their registered email address.
4. Open the password reset email.
5. Click the password reset link.
6. Create a new password.
7. Return to the login page and sign in.

## Password Reset Email

* Password reset emails normally arrive within 5 minutes.
* Customers should check their spam or junk folder.
* Password reset links expire after 30 minutes.
* Customers can request a new reset link if the previous link expires.

## Account Lockout

* Accounts are temporarily locked after 5 unsuccessful login attempts.
* The temporary lock normally lasts 30 minutes.
* Customers can attempt login again after the lock period.
* Repeated account lockouts should be referred to technical support.

## Login Troubleshooting

Customers experiencing login problems should:

1. Verify their registered email address.
2. Check that Caps Lock is disabled.
3. Reset their password if necessary.
4. Clear browser cache and cookies.
5. Try another browser or device.

## Account Security

* Customers must never share passwords or OTPs with support agents.
* AI support agents must not request passwords or OTPs.
* Suspected unauthorized access must be escalated to human support.
* Account recovery requiring identity verification must be handled by authorized support staff.
