# The AI Demo Store – Shipping Policy

## Standard Delivery

* Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within major cities.
* Delivery to rural locations takes 5–8 business days.
* Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays.
* Delivery timelines begin after the order is dispatched.

## Express Delivery

* Express delivery takes 1–2 business days in eligible locations.
* Express shipping costs ₹149.
* Express shipping is not available for all postal codes.

## Shipping Charges

* Orders worth ₹999 or more qualify for free standard shipping.
* Orders below ₹999 incur a standard shipping fee of ₹79.

## Order Tracking

* Customers receive a tracking link by email after dispatch.
* Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to update.
* Customers can track orders through My Account > My Orders.

## Delayed Deliveries

* Customers should contact support if an order is delayed by more than 2 business days beyond the estimated delivery date.
* The logistics team will investigate delayed shipments.
* Shipment investigations normally take 24–48 business hours.
* Customers should provide their Order ID when reporting delays.

## Lost Packages

* A package may be classified as lost after a logistics investigation.
* Refunds or replacements for lost packages require confirmation from the logistics team.
* AI support agents cannot independently approve lost-package refunds or replacements.
