# The AI Demo Store – Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Refund Eligibility

* Eligible products may be returned within 7 calendar days of delivery.
* Products must be returned with their original packaging and accessories.
* Items damaged during delivery are eligible for review under the Product Return Policy.
* Certain hygiene products and personalized items are non-returnable unless defective.

## Order Cancellation

* Customers may cancel orders before dispatch.
* Once an order is dispatched, customers must follow the return process.
* Cancellation requests can be submitted through My Account > My Orders.

## Refund Processing

* Approved refunds to the original payment method are processed within 5–7 business days.
* Bank processing times may vary.
* Refund processing begins after the return is approved.
* Refunds are not automatically approved when a customer submits a request.

## Refund Status

* Customers can check refund status through My Account > My Orders.
* Customers should contact support if an approved refund has not arrived after 7 business days.
* Customers must provide their Order ID for refund investigations.

## Refund Escalation

* Refund requests involving disputed payments must be escalated to human support.
* Refunds delayed beyond the stated processing timeline require investigation.
* AI support agents cannot approve refunds or initiate financial transactions.
